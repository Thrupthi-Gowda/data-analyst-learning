Cyber Security is the practice of protecting systems, networks, and personal information from cyber attacks. Learn about online threats and how to stay safe.

Common Cyber Threats

  • Phishing Attacks
  • Malware and Viruses
  • Ransomware

Cyber Safety Tips

  1. Use strong passwords.
  2. Enable Two-Factor Authentication.
  3. Never click suspicious links.
  4. Keep software updated.
  5. Use antivirus protection.
